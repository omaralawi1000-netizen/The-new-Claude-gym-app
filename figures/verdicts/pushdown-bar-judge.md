# Judge verdict: pushdown_bar (130f, 30fps)

SCORE: 8.4 / 10

Viewed: 12-frame contact sheet; zoomed crops at f0, 20, 36, 50, 64, 80, 100; hand crops f0/f64; f40-42 triple.

## What works
- Elbows stay pinned at the ribs; forearms sweep from horizontal (f0) to vertical-down lockout (~f60-64); bar reaches the upper thighs. Overhand grip reads.
- Slight lean and head are stable; no head wobble. Loop is clean (f129 ~ f0).
- Triceps glow sits on the lateral/long head at the back of the near upper arm, correct muscle, saturated red, no navy/blue tint.
- f40/41/42: no pop or flicker, no specks, no grey patches seen. Palette is neutral warm-grey, not blue.

## Flaws, most visible on a phone first
1. Cable grazes/rides the chest and shoulder outline, f~30-64 (gate: 59 tangent runs, longest 72 px). In f36/f50 the line hugs the near pec/shoulder edge and at f50-64 slips behind the torso, then reappears at the bar. It reads as the cable clipping the body. Most visible flaw.
   Fix: move the pulley anchor ~8-10 cm further out along camera-right (away from the torso) and/or rotate the whole rig ~5 deg so the line clears the chest silhouette by >=6 px at f30-64. Alternatively draw the cable in front of the body with a 1 px soft light edge so it does not look merged with the outline.
2. Cable is not vertical at the top of the stroke. At f0-20 it runs straight down to the bar centre (good) but between f36-64 it slants hard (about 15 deg) while the bar is at the thighs; looks like the pulley is too far forward/behind the user. A real high-pulley pushdown has a mild slant only.
   Fix: shift pulley ~10 cm toward the user's line (above the hands at mid-stroke), keeping the bar-centre attachment.
3. Hands at lockout (f64-100) are small, flat grey blobs: fingers wrap on the near hand but the far hand shows a thin wrist and a blocky fist; at f100 the far hand looks pinched under the bar. Not quite Apple-grade.
   Fix: add 5-8 deg finger curl and knuckle separation on the far hand at f60-110; increase hand scale ~4 percent and keep the thumb under the bar.
4. Grip drift (~13 mm, ~2 px) is not visible at phone size. Ignore, or lock hand parent to the bar at the mid-stroke.
5. Bar end-caps: dull flat ends, no chamfer; the cable clip (silver cylinder) is the only bright element and looks slightly glued on. Minor.
   Fix: add a 1 mm chamfer and a small swivel ring above the clip.
6. Body is static: little visible breathing or weight shift at 130 frames; knees and feet appear locked. Minor, reads as mannequin.
   Fix: add 4-5 mm chest rise (2 breaths per loop) and 1 deg pelvis sway over the loop.
7. Far-limb slivers and dimmed gap ends (listed by gate) are not noticeable at phone size.

## Notes
- Figure is small in frame (about 40 percent height) and a big grey pillar fills the right third; bar/hand detail is hard to read on a phone. Crop/zoom the camera ~15 percent toward the torso.
- Do not inflate: the rope version scored 8.6-9.0; this is slightly below because of the cable/chest clip.
